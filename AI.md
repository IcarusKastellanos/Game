# Witchcraft Sim — AI context

Witchcraft Sim is a multiplayer browser life-sim / RPG set in a grounded folk-magic world: players live as practicing witches, keep apartments, walk a shared town, and work magic through historical-feeling craft rather than arcade combat. The presentation is deliberately 16-bit / pixel-art (tiles and icons are authored at 32×32; character walk frames are 32×48 with eight directions). Gameplay centers on gathering and using ingredients, casting at altars and through pocket workings, tending wards and aura, managing hunger and stamina, and growing a practitioner identity over time—not clearing dungeons.

Systems are content-driven. Spells, ingredients, shops, maps, dialogues, quests, progression curves, recognition tiers, and licenses live as JSON under `content/` and are synced into PostgreSQL for persistence and admin editing. Magical traditions (e.g. Traditional European) gate affinity and teacher lessons; public vs spiritual reputation, practitioner levels, licensure marks, and a personal grimoire track advancement. NPCs are first-class: they have schedules and presence, per-player bonds, gifts and social gestures, shops, quests, discovery/rumor pipelines, and authored or procedural portraits plus walk/idle sheets. Multiplayer rooms (apartment, town) sync movement and world state; chat, HUD chrome (ledger, grimoire, spellbook, social circle), and in-world speech bubbles cover social play.

Technically this is a TypeScript npm workspaces monorepo: `client` (Phaser 3 + Vite), `server` (Express + Colyseus + Prisma), `shared` (types, constants, validation, NPC/progression logic), and `admin-client` (Vite content/map/NPC/item editors). Prefer changing shared contracts and content registries over hardcoding behavior in scenes; keep art sizes aligned with `TILE_SIZE` / icon and NPC sheet constants in `shared`. When editing, treat admin tools and runtime as one pipeline—sprites, icons, and NPC data authored in admin must match the pixel counts and sheet layouts the client and server already validate.
